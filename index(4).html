Icebreaker

WHY ARE WE DOING THIS?

This is a short icebreaker designed to help us understand ourselves and each other better.

We will explore what we feel our job expects from us, and how we are when we can be ourselves.

This exercise is not a final answer about who you are. It is a starting point for reflection, awareness and conversation.

HOW TO ANSWER

In each group of four statements, you have seven checks to distribute freely.

You can put all seven checks on one statement, or split them across several statements. Use exactly seven checks in each group.

Each group is a fresh choice. When you start a new group, look at the four statements with fresh eyes.

EXAMPLE

7 checks can be distributed in many ways:

7 on one statement or 4 + 3 or 3 + 2 + 2 or 2 + 2 + 2 + 1.
