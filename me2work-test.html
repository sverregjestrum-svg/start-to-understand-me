WHY ARE WE DOING THIS?

This is a simple way to understand yourself and others.

We all have different strengths, preferences and ways of working.

The tool helps you put words to your strengths and ways of working. It creates a shared language for understanding ourselves and others, helping us recognize what we have in common and how we may think, communicate and work differently.

When team members understand each other’s natural strengths and where they may need to stretch, it becomes easier to bring out the best in one another.

HOW TO ANSWER

In each group of four statements, you have seven checks to distribute.

You can put all seven checks on one statement, or split them across several statements. Use exactly seven checks in each group.

Each group is a fresh choice. When you start a new group, look at the four statements with fresh eyes.

EXAMPLE

7 checks can be distributed in many ways:

7 on one statement or 4 + 3 or 3 + 2 + 2 or 2 + 2 + 2 + 1.

EXPECTATIONS vs. BEING MYSELF

What does my job require from me — and how does that compare with what comes naturally to me?

Natural
This shows how you tend to be when you can be yourself — the ways of working that feel most natural to you.

Job expectation
This shows how you feel your job requires you to be — the ways of working you believe are expected from you.

What can you learn?

The difference between your natural strengths and your job expectations is that your job may require you to use strengths that do not come as naturally to you. Meeting these expectations may sometimes require you to stretch a little further and step outside your comfort zone. A larger difference may mean that you are relying more on strengths that take extra effort and energy.

Your natural profile shows how you tend to be when you can be yourself — the ways of working that feel most natural to you. It can therefore give you a clearer picture of your “real you”.
