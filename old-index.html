HOW TO GET ON THE SAME WAVELENGTH

Different people bring different strengths. Here are some simple ways to work well with each one.

🔵 Explorer

Give the Explorer space to share ideas and be heard. Capture good ideas in an idea bank, so they can be quality-checked and considered when the time is right.

🟢 Doer

Ask what they have achieved, what is on their to-do list, and how they are progressing towards their goals.

🔴 Organizer

Let the Organizer explain how the work should be done, how the plan has been thought through, and how it meets relevant rules and procedures.

🟡 Unifier

Ask about people’s strengths, individual development, how the team can work even better together, and how they can have a positive influence on others.
